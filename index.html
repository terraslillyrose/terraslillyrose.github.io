How to detect AI-generated images in order to protect oneself from them?

#mathematics #imageprocessing #python #AI · 2026 · Team of 5 · Prépa t² (tutor: J.-M. Hok)

Context & problem
AI-generated images are becoming so realistic that they can no longer be spotted by eye (six-fingered hands are a thing of the past). They fuel disinformation and propaganda. Our question: is there a way to detect them objectively?
My role
I was part of the computer science team: I helped design and code the Python program (grayscale conversion, noise extraction with a Gaussian filter, FFT and spectrum display), ran the tests on real and AI-generated images, and published the commented code on GitHub. I also took part in the research phase and in the preparation of the report and the oral presentation.
Methods & tools
Digital image modelling (pixels, RGB, grayscale); convolution filters; 2D Fourier transform (FFT) in Python/NumPy; noise extraction with a Gaussian filter; log-scale spectrum visualisation; literature review (including work by Hany Farid).
Outcomes
We built a working program that displays the grayscale image, its noise/high frequencies and the Fourier spectrum. Our tests did not show a clearly distinguishable pattern between real and AI images, which we explained by the rapid evolution of generative AI and the limited literature. We also explored complementary approaches (watermarking, EU AI Act, detection tools).
Lessons learned
Turning theoretical mathematics into a concrete tool; managing information (knowing what to keep and what to drop); teamwork at a distance; critical thinking about images.
